## High-stakes restrictions / legal review required
- CRD VI Article 21c has rules on third-country banks servicing EU customers, including when a third-country bank needs an authorized EU branch; solicitation via someone acting on the bank's behalf can undermine the own-initiative exception: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32024L1619
- Marketing Swissquote/AMINA/Xapo to EU residents via paid introductions must be reviewed independently; a bank saying EU customers may *on their own initiative* apply is NOT proof that PWC may solicit EU leads.
- In the Hong Kong bank's official product page: "Please don't share your information with third parties, or engage with any intermediaries to open an account." Do not portray this as a partnership.
- Belize regulator also warns about similarly named **unlicensed** "International Bank Belize" and "Offshore Bank of Belize"; cross-check exact legal name against primary regulator registry: https://www.centralbank.org.bz/detail-pages/news/2026/03/17/warning-notice---international-bank-belize
- A platform offering an **account**, an **electronic money wallet**, an **investment brokerage account**, or **crypto custody** must state which licensed entity holds which assets and which compensation scheme applies. Crypto balances generally do NOT carry deposit insurance simply because an affiliated bank is regulated.
- Do not promise tax invisibility, digital-euro evasion, KYC avoidance, guaranteed account approvals, or returns.
- No money custody, bank application submission, passport/tax document collection, prospect forwarding or paid introductions until relevant Bulgarian and host-country legal sign-off, independent security/privacy review and bank-signed agreements.
