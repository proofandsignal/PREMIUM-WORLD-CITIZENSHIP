## 01 | Who we are

**Premium World Citizenship by TrendSolutions** is an emerging research and client-navigation concept designed to make international options easier to understand. We aim to bridge verified public information, clear preliminary inquiries and introductions to independently qualified institutions — **only when compliant agreements and legal authorization permit**.

Our approach is **source-first**: any claim about eligibility, bank-account availability, fees, tax treatment or residency must be supported by an authoritative source and human review, rather than reused promotional material.

**What is demonstrable today:** a private, synthetic FAQ and locally generated inquiry-draft prototype, two product routes, browser automation tests, and research directories. We do **not** yet have live AI, institutional integrations, partner agreements or client applications.

## 02 | Explore four pathways

**Global Banking Intelligence**  
Compare published banking options, eligibility restrictions, onboarding procedures, account types and fee disclosures. Bank-led KYC, acceptance and account management stay entirely with the authorized institution.

**Global Mobility Research**  
Understand published residency and citizenship frameworks and identify official agency or licensed professional channels. No immigration advice or approvals are offered by the project.

**International Business**  
Map international incorporation, corporate support and cross-border payment services using verified providers and transparent eligibility information.

**Tax & Compliance Orientation**  
Educational content on cross-border obligations, documentation and professional referral pathways. Jurisdiction-specific advice belongs to the qualified practitioner.

5. **Human approval** — no partner promotion, commercial terms, production merge or public release without independent review.
