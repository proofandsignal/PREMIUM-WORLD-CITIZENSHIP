## Questions to ask in writing
1. Does your programme accept a Bulgaria/EU-based informational publisher or consultancy without a sector-specific licence, and what specific acts can we legally perform?
2. Which products may we introduce, to residents of which countries, and under what licensed entity?
3. Who retains full regulated advice, KYC/AML, client onboarding, filings and liability?
4. What is the commercial basis (per referred qualified lead / paying client / revenue share), currency, clawback/refunds, attribution, disclosure and payout schedule?
5. Are commercial terms, partner agreements, brand use rights and marketing claims available for review **before** public promotion?
6. Can the prospective client start directly on your official website without sharing personal data with PWC or Tax Genie? Is a consent-based warm introduction possible later after privacy legal signoff?
7. What verified sources, product exclusions, terms, and update notices may we publish in the PWC directory?
