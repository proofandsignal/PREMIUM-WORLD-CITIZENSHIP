**Status:** Private concept / engineering preparation. No public deployment or client onboarding.

**Mission:** Explain lawful international banking, mobility, residency, citizenship and business options with source-led guidance. No legal, tax, banking or immigration advice or guaranteed results.

## Product boundaries
- **Premium World Citizenship** owns its own website, knowledge content and prospective partner workflow.
- **Tax Genie — by Proof & Signal** remains independent in [TAX-Genie](https://github.com/proofandsignal/TAX-Genie); its security, QA, legal, merge and deployment gates remain unchanged.
- A proposed **Shared AI Concierge** provides brand-aware general FAQ, eligibility *pre-screening questions only* and separate intake draft workflows. It is not a licensed professional, and it must not make final eligibility, refund, banking or immigration decisions.
- No passports, bank statements, tax identifiers, invoices or other sensitive documents in chatbot/ManyChat.
- Do not send or persist personal client data until privacy notice, lawful basis, retention, access controls, security and vendor agreements have been reviewed.
- No automatic outreach or partner status change. DRAFT ≠ SENT.
